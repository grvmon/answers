Key takeaways

  • A useful consultant is a filter and a translator, not a tour guide who only repeats brochure lines.
  • The core work runs from your brief to the shortlist, site visits, document checks, cost clarity and follow-through after booking.
  • For RERA-registered projects, agents who facilitate sale or purchase must be registered under section 9 of the RERA Act, 2016.

Real estate consultant Bangalore: the job, stage by stage

You'll meet three roles that sound alike: a builder sales executive, a channel partner who only pushes inventory, and a consultant who starts from your constraints. This page is about the third.

Clarifying the brief before any site visit

The real work starts before you see a sample flat. A consultant should pin down:

  • Budget range including registration, GST (where applicable), parking, floor rise and other add-ons, not only the “base price” slide.
  • Must-haves versus nice-to-haves (schools, commute to ITPL or Outer Ring Road, low-rise versus high-rise, ready-to-move versus under construction).
  • Who will live there (self-use, parents, NRI remote purchase) and when you need keys.
  • Risk appetite on construction stage, land title complexity and civic jurisdiction (BBMP / Greater Bengaluru Authority areas versus panchayat pockets on the East fringe).

If someone skips this and opens a WhatsApp folder of “hot deals”, they're selling inventory, not advising.

Building a shortlist for East Bengaluru

In East Bengaluru the same ticket price can buy very different living experiences across Whitefield, Brookefield, Kadugodi, Hoodi, Mahadevapura, Bellandur, Panathur, Sarjapur Road, Varthur, Gunjur, Budigere Cross, Hoskote and Old Madras Road. The consultant’s job is to shrink that map to a few projects that fit your brief and be straight about the trade-offs, like commute against quiet, social infrastructure against price per sq ft, or delivery risk against a discount.

They should also tell you what they won't show you, and why. That list of rejects is often more useful than a long brochure list.

Checking project and agent basics against RERA

Before you pay a token, your consultant should help you confirm that the project appears on the Karnataka RERA portal and that the person facilitating the sale is registered where the Act requires it. Section 9 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 requires real estate agents facilitating sale or purchase in a registered project to obtain registration. Section 10 sets out agent duties, including not facilitating unregistered projects and not using unfair trade practices.

A consultant isn't your lawyer, but they shouldn't rush you past obvious gaps either: missing RERA details, vague possession language, or pressure to “book today or lose the unit”.

Site visits that answer real questions

A site visit isn't a photo walk. You want answers on:

  • Access roads at peak hour, not only Sunday morning.
  • What is already built nearby versus what is still a brochure promise.
  • Sample flat versus actual tower finishing standards.
  • Who maintains common areas once residents move in.

A consultant who knows East corridors will warn you about choke points near ITPL, ORR junctions, Sarjapur Road and Whitefield Main Road without pretending traffic will “vanish next year”.

Cost clarity beyond the base price

Buyers lose money in the gap between the advertised base price and what actually leaves their account at registration. A consultant should walk you through typical add-ons and point you to a proper cost sheet from the developer. Exact GST, stamp duty and registration figures depend on the unit and current state rules, so treat any verbal “all-in” number as incomplete until you see a written working.

Booking, agreement and follow-through

Once you shortlist a unit, the consultant coordinates with the developer’s sales team on availability, payment schedule and document pack. They should push for time to read the agreement for sale, not celebrate a same-day token as a win. After booking, good follow-through means explaining demand notices, sense-checking construction updates and helping NRI buyers who need remote coordination.

What a consultant should not claim to replace

  • A property lawyer for title opinion and agreement mark-up.
  • A chartered accountant for tax structuring.
  • A home-loan underwriter for sanction certainty.
  • A civic authority for Khata, occupancy or property tax status.

Think of the consultant as the person who keeps things organised and honest, so those specialists get asked the right questions.

Buyer checklist: what to do next

  1. Put your brief on one page: all-in budget, commute anchors, ready versus under construction, and the amenities you can't do without.
  2. Ask every consultant for their Karnataka RERA agent registration details, then check them yourself on rera.karnataka.gov.in.
  3. On the very first call, ask: “Who pays you on the projects you'll show me?”
  4. Insist on a shortlist of 3-5 projects max for your corridor, and ask why the others didn't make it.
  5. Before any token, pull up the project page on Karnataka RERA and jot down the registration number, promoter name and proposed completion details.
  6. Get the cost sheet in writing, because the base price alone won't tell you much. Our note on hidden costs beyond base price covers the rest.
  7. Unless your document checks are already done, sleep on the booking for at least one night.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a real estate consultant if I already like one project?

Not always. If you have already done RERA checks, cost math and a lawyer review, you may only need help on negotiation logistics. A consultant still helps when you want a second shortlist or an independent pushback on sales claims.

Can a consultant help NRI buyers who can't travel to Bengaluru?

Yes, many East Bangalore purchases for NRIs are coordinated remotely with video walkthroughs, document sharing and a local Power of Attorney process where needed. The consultant should still insist on the same RERA and paperwork checks you would do in person.

Is a consultant the same as a broker?

In daily speech people mix the words. Under RERA, the legal category is “real estate agent” when someone facilitates sale or purchase in a registered project. Titles on visiting cards matter less than registration, disclosure and the quality of advice. See consultant vs broker vs advisory.

Will a consultant get me a lower price than the builder desk?

Sometimes they can surface inventory or payment plans you would not see alone. There is no guaranteed discount percentage. Treat any “special rate only today” claim as a sales tactic until it appears on an official cost sheet.

What East Bangalore areas does a local consultant usually cover?

For acreNkey, the focus is Whitefield, Sarjapur Road, Varthur, Gunjur, Marathahalli, KR Puram, Budigere Cross, Hoskote, Old Madras Road, Panathur, Bellandur, Kadugodi, Brookefield, Mahadevapura, Hoodi and ITPL. Depth in a few corridors beats thin coverage across the whole city.
